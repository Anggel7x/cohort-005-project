# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses and can ask for their money back within a short window.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a Purchase, which the team reviews by hand. It is pending until it is approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Chargeback, Reversal

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a learner may make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Chargeback**:
Reserved term. It means a dispute a learner raises through their bank against a payment, and the platform does not model it today. Never use it for a Refund Request.
